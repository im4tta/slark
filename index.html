Image tests

    

      

        
No change

        
Control group — the watermarked PNG, untouched. Should always pass.

JPEG re-compression

        
Saves as JPEG quality 85 and reopens it. Lossy DCT compression rewrites low-order bits wholesale — LSB tags die here.

        Stamp an image first
      

      

        
Resize 75% → back

        
Shrinks to 75% then upscales back. Resampling invents new pixels; the original bit positions are gone.

        Stamp an image first
      

      

        
Brightness +6%

        
A gentle exposure bump through a canvas filter. Tiny for eyes, fatal for least-significant bits.

        Stamp an image first
      

      

        
Cover ~18% with a box

        
Pastes an opaque rectangle over part of the image. Redundant copies mean the tag often survives local damage like this.

        Stamp an image first
      

    


    
These are the mechanics as implemented here, tested against your actual content in your browser right now — not claims about what any specific product does. For text, if you need certainty for a specific destination, test it directly with slark.check() after pasting the output back out. For images: keep them as PNG end-to-end; every lossy hop (JPEG, WebP, screenshots, most social platforms) strips LSB payloads by design.

  Detects only Slark's own tag formats — not watermarks from other AI systems or tools.
